What the number means and how it moves

← Standings

The short version

Every player has a rating. Win and it goes up; lose and it goes down. Beating someone rated above you moves it more than beating someone below you. The scoreline does not change the size of the swing — only who won.

The site calls the number ELO. Under the hood it is a Glicko-2 rating, which also tracks how confident the system is in your number. That confidence is what the provisional badge and the hidden RD value represent.

Your number

Rating

The headline figure on your profile and the ladder. Everyone starts at 500.

Provisional

Shown while the system is still unsure about your level. Play more rated matches and it clears.

This week

How much your rating has changed in the last seven days, not your total for the season.

Reading a profile

Last 5 / Recent

Your latest results, newest first. W L W W W means you won four of the last five. “Recent” appears until there are five results to show.

Streak

Consecutive wins or losses. +6 streak is six wins in a row.

Next rank

How many rating points you need to reach the next tier. The bar fills as you close the gap.

Seasons

The 1v1 ladder runs in seasons. Closing a season saves a standings snapshot and resets 1v1 ratings for the next one. Other formats keep their ratings. Archived seasons stay readable from the Seasons page.

Reporting

Signed-in players can report a result from the Report page. The form previews the rating swing first; submit once. The same pairing cannot be reported twice in a row, and reporting is rate-limited.
